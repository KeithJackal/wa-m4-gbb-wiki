# Hop up

An important component for an airsoft replica, without it your BBs would just not fly properly.

Fortunately with WA systems, and clones, you can still get newly made hop up units. Notably VFC is the only company who did not change the spec when they derived their replicas from WA.

{% hint style="info" %}
Getting a VFC chamber is important if you want to guarantee that modern hop units fit. Not all WA clone chambers are to spec and specifically Golden Eagle ones are a bit too narrow.
{% endhint %}

Install a VFC AR chamber and use any hop up unit of your choice.

Typical recommendations are

* 4UAD Magnus Pro
* GHK 2022 GBB spec hop up\*
* Maple Leaf

\*Certain new Golden Eagle rifles ship with a clone of the GHK hop up.
